# EB300 CAD-2 mesh provenance

EB300 printed parts are by **ToolBoxRobotics**, [Thingiverse6283770](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6283770), [manufacturer](https://toolboxrobotics.com/robotic-arm-eb300). The original archive notice is preserved verbatim in [EB300 license](licenses/ToolBoxRobotics-EB300-LICENSE.txt).

The EBG-20 gripper parts are by **ToolBoxRobotics**, [Thingiverse6460227](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6460227). Its distinct original notice is preserved in [EBG20 license](licenses/ToolBoxRobotics-EBG20-LICENSE.txt); it is not replaced by the arm's license.

Visual copies were prepared with the stock Trimesh normal-repair/STL-export API; collision copies are stock convex hulls. No source holes were filled. Source paths, hashes, transformations and changes are recorded in `docs/cad-reviews/2026-11-14-cad2/prepared-source-meshes.json`. These local derived assets retain their upstream notices; preparing them here does not publish or relicense them.

Native tubes were provided in the owner's assembly. The supplied manufacturer README specifies **3-inch and2-inch black ABS pipe**, not steel. Their measured native geometric dimensions are used, while default CAD steel density is not used as measured mass. `prepared-native-meshes.json` records exports and hashes.

Phone envelope, mounts, output flange proxy and typing tool are local nominal CAD design data, not ToolBoxRobotics production drawings. Xiaomi [Redmi8A specifications](https://www.mi.com/in/redmi-8a/specs/) supply only its body dimensions and188g mass; camera calibration is not supplied by those dimensions.
